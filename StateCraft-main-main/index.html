The 8 Core Governance Criteria

Predefined metrics and functional responsibilities modeled for all 28 states of India:

🎓

1. Education

Literacy rates, higher education GER, school transformation, and teacher ratios.

🏥

2. Healthcare

NITI Health index, infant mortality rate (IMR), hospital beds, and universal coverage.

⚡

3. Infrastructure

Highway density, ports, clean energy grids, airports, and urban mass rapid transit.

👥

4. Population

Demographic scale, urbanization percentage, tribal demographics, and welfare reach.

🏭

5. Industrial Development

Gross industrial output, Ease of Doing Business rank, SEZs, and export manufacturing.

🛡️

6. Law Enforcement

Public safety index, police personnel per lakh, emergency response, and cyber defense.

💰

7. Per Capita Income

Net State Domestic Product (NSDP) per capita, growth momentum, and wage prosperity.

📊

8. State Debt

Debt-to-GSDP ratio, fiscal deficit prudential limits, and sovereign borrowing solvency.
